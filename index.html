OUR CRAFT

Marinate. Season.
Dry. Slice.

The flavour starts before the meat ever reaches the dryer. Wildeveld uses a savoury marinade and traditional biltong-style seasoning, then gives each batch the time it needs to dry properly.

The result is biltong with deep flavour, a firm outer texture and the satisfying chew people expect from proper biltong.
